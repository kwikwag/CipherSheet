1. Use of the Add-on

CipherSheet provides a tool to encrypt and decrypt specific cells within your Google Sheets™ spreadsheet. Encryption is performed client-side using asymmetric ECDH key pairs — each user's keypair is generated in their own browser. Collaborators can encrypt data for each other using registered public keys without sharing private key material.

2. User Responsibility and Data Loss

CipherSheet uses a zero-knowledge architecture for key handling. We do not store, backup, or have any access to your encryption keys. Your private key is generated in your browser and never transmitted to any server. If you lose your unlock password and have no passkey enrolled, data encrypted with your key may become permanently inaccessible.
