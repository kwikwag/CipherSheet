How to Use CipherSheet

1. Generate a Key

Click Generate key in the sidebar. A personal keypair is created right in your browser — your public key is registered so teammates can encrypt for you, while your private key never leaves your device.

2. Protect Values

Select a cell, type your sensitive data into the CipherSheet box, pick recipients from your list of collaborators, and click Protect. The cell becomes ciphertext instantly — only the recipients you chose can read it, each from their own sidebar.

3. Secure Your Key

Your private key is locked with a strong, auto-generated password. Save it in your password manager — or enroll a passkey for one-tap unlock. Because no one else holds it, lose the password with no passkey and the data is unrecoverable, so keep it safe.

Share team secrets without sharing the risk

Keep API keys, passwords, and other sensitive values in the same Sheet your team already works in. Add the teammates who need access as recipients — everyone reads the values they're entitled to, and no one (not even Google) ever sees the plaintext on the server. No separate vault, no copy-pasting between tools.

How Your Data Stays Private

CipherSheet is built on a zero-knowledge architecture: your secrets are sealed in your browser before they ever reach the cloud. We couldn't read your data even if we wanted to — and neither can Google.

Encrypted in your browser

All encryption and decryption happen locally via the Web Crypto API. Only ciphertext and public keys are ever sent to Google's servers.

Your key stays yours

Your private key never leaves your device. Only your public key is shared, so collaborators can encrypt for you — never decrypt as you.

Truly in your control

No CipherSheet account, no server-side copy of your keys, no backdoor. Open-source and auditable on GitHub.

⚠️ If you lose your unlock password and passkey, it's effectively impossible to recover your data. Keep your password secure.

Why We Need Permissions

When you install CipherSheet, Google asks you to authorize two scopes — the minimum needed, and nothing more:

  • Current spreadsheet only (auth/spreadsheets.currentonly): read the selected cell's value, write the encrypted payload back when you click "Protect", and store your public key so collaborators can encrypt for you. CipherSheet can never see your other Sheets or any other Google data.
  • Add-on sidebar & menu (auth/script.container.ui): show the CipherSheet sidebar, menu, and dialogs inside Google Sheets.

Passkey unlock runs in a small popup of static HTML served from CipherSheet's server. It exchanges only the data needed to unlock your key in your browser and never receives your spreadsheet content.
